TDF Records Account Privacy Notice

This notice explains how TDF Records handles information used to create, secure, and operate an account across its web application, mobile application, and connected services.

Key points

  • TDF Records does not sell personal data.
  • Role and permission changes are separately authorized and audited.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us.

1. Information handled

  • Identity and contact details supplied during signup or profile updates.
  • Authentication, session, consent-version, role, permission, and security records.
  • Content and transaction data entered through authorized TDF Records workflows.
  • Operational logs, device context, and diagnostics used for security and support.
  • Optional camera, photo, or location data only when a related feature is used and permitted.

2. How information is used

  • To create and authenticate your account and preserve your selected preferences.
  • To provide workflows that your approved roles and permissions allow.
  • To prevent misuse, investigate failures, and maintain reliable service.
  • To provide support and meet applicable legal, accounting, and recordkeeping duties.

3. Sharing and retention

Information is shared only with authorized TDF Records personnel and service providers needed to operate requested features. Records are retained only as needed for service, security, support, and applicable business or legal obligations.

4. Security and choices

  • Keep credentials private and sign out from devices you no longer control.
  • Deny optional device permissions when you do not want the related feature.
  • Ask us to review, correct, export, or delete eligible account information.
