Solicitar eliminación de mi cuenta

La página muestra el método disponible para solicitar la eliminación. Si el formulario está habilitado, inicia sesión con la cuenta que deseas eliminar y confirma la solicitud. También puedes escribir a info@tdfrecords.net. No envíes contraseñas ni documentos de identidad. La solicitud abarca la cuenta completa y sus datos personales, incluido el contenido publicado.

El equipo verificará que la cuenta te pertenece y gestionará la solicitud. Si algún registro debe conservarse por obligaciones legales, fiscales, de seguridad o por una disputa, te explicaremos cuál y por qué. Nuestro objetivo es tramitar la solicitud en 30 días, sujeto a esas verificaciones y obligaciones.

Desde la app: Acerca de → Support and legal → Data Deletion → Solicitar eliminación de mi cuenta.

1. How to request deletion

  • Open the account-deletion page and follow the currently available request method.
  • When using the authenticated form, check the account shown, confirm deletion of the entire account and submit.
  • The team processes deletion manually and confirms completion through the verified account contact. Request received does not mean deletion completed.
  • The privacy contact remains available for account-deletion requests, questions or requests concerning specific data.

2. What account deletion covers

  • Your entire TDF account, profile and associated personal information where TDF Records controls that data.
  • User-generated content, including published posts, comments, photos and reviews, except legally retained records explained below.
  • Inventory photos or uploads submitted through the app.
  • QR or vCard exchange records associated with your account or Party ID.
  • Optional location-derived venue search data tied to service requests, where retained.

3. What may be retained

Some records may need to be retained for tax, accounting, fraud prevention, security, dispute handling, or other legal obligations. If that applies, we will explain what cannot be deleted and why.

4. Response timeline

We aim to confirm receipt and process deletion requests within 30 days, subject to identity verification and any legal retention duties.
