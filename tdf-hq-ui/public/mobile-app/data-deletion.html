Eliminar tu cuenta TDF y sus datos

Solicita la eliminación desde el correo asociado a tu cuenta escribiendo a info@tdfrecords.net. Indica si deseas eliminar la cuenta completa o datos concretos. No envíes contraseñas, códigos de acceso, datos de pago ni documentos de identidad en la solicitud inicial.

El equipo verificará que la cuenta te pertenece y gestionará la solicitud. Si algún registro debe conservarse por obligaciones legales, fiscales, de seguridad o por una disputa, te explicaremos cuál y por qué. Nuestro objetivo es tramitar la solicitud en 30 días, sujeto a esas verificaciones y obligaciones.

Desde la app: Acerca de → Support and legal → Data Deletion.

1. What to send us

  • The email address tied to your TDF account, so we can locate it.
  • Do not send passwords, login codes, payment details or identity documents in the initial request.
  • The platform you used: iPhone or Android.
  • A short description of whether you want account deletion, workflow cleanup, or specific content removed.

2. What we can review for deletion

  • Profile and contact information associated with your mobile access where TDF Records controls that data.
  • Inventory photos or uploads submitted through the app.
  • QR or vCard exchange records associated with your account or Party ID.
  • Optional location-derived venue search data tied to service requests, where retained.

3. What may be retained

Some records may need to be retained for tax, accounting, fraud prevention, security, dispute handling, or other legal obligations. If that applies, we will explain what cannot be deleted and why.

4. Response timeline

We aim to confirm receipt and process deletion requests within 30 days, subject to identity verification and any legal retention duties.
